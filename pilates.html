Frequently asked questions

Do I need previous experience?

No. We start with a quick assessment and adapt the class to your level.

What if I have an injury?

Tell us when you book. If needed, we’ll suggest checking with your doctor or physio first.

What should I wear?

Comfortable clothes and non-slip socks. We provide the rest.

Can I cancel a class?

Yes, up to 12 hours before at no charge.
