Your audience splits into two groups with different questions. The page speaks to both without either one scrolling past their answer.

Turn a backyard into a system

Most of your students start with a lawn, a few pots, or a patch that never quite worked. They want a plan they can follow this season, not a theory course.

  • Site reading: sun, water flow, and soil, explained on their own plot
  • A planting plan sized to a real backyard, not a demonstration farm
  • Follow-along sessions across a full growing season
  • Small groups, so every question gets answered

Design a plot that keeps itself going

Community and school gardens need a design that survives turnover — new volunteers, new students, new seasons — without starting over each year.

  • A shared planting map anyone on the team can follow
  • Low-maintenance layers that reduce weekly upkeep
  • Hands-on sessions the whole group can join
  • A simple handover guide for the next season's volunteers
