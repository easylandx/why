Why field instructors are different.

Our instructors don't teach from a slide deck in a clean classroom. They've packed wound kits by headlamp, splinted fractures on scree slopes, and triaged patients in collapsed structures. Every course is built around one rule: if it can't be done with dirty hands and a shaking flashlight, it doesn't make the curriculum.

We certify instructors to teach wilderness first aid (WFA/WFR-level) and urban disaster response — improvised evacuation, mass-casualty triage, and psychological first aid under chaos.
