Frequently asked questions

How long does a plan take?

It depends on size. A simple plan can be ready a few days after the diagnosis.

Does it cover self-protection regulations?

We adapt it to the applicable local rules. Each case is checked against official sources.

Do you run drills with children or the public?

Yes, in a format suited to each group and always calmly.

What if we already have a plan?

We review it, simplify it and rehearse it.
