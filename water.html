About me

I spent twenty years designing water treatment plants in a city where every drop is counted. Today I help people, families and communities in several countries get safe water without relying on the grid: collecting it, cleaning it and looking after it.

I won't sell you equipment you don't need. I'll explain what is in your water, what is really required and how to keep it running.
