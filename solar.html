Why it matters

The grid is one point of failure. Solar removes it.

Storms and outages

Regional blackouts can last days. A stored-energy system keeps lights, comms, and refrigeration running independent of utility repair crews.

No fuel supply chain

Generators need gasoline or propane, which run out and require storage. Panels keep producing as long as the sun rises.

Silent and scalable

Start with a single panel and power bank, then grow toward a full home battery bank — no need to buy the end state on day one.

The system

Four parts, in this order, every time.

1. Panel

Converts sunlight to DC power. Rated in watts — more watts, faster charging.

2. Charge controller

Regulates voltage from the panel so it charges the battery safely, without overcharging.

3. Battery bank

Stores energy for night use and cloudy days. Sized in amp-hours (Ah) at a given voltage.

4. Inverter

Converts stored DC into standard AC so it can run household devices and outlets.
