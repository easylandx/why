The studio

Storing and transporting

Basic tips. How long a cake lasts depends on the cake, the temperature and the journey.

In the fridge

Cakes with cream or fruit are kept cold. Take it out a little while before serving.

Away from heat

Sun and hot cars melt the cream and spoil the design.

For the journey

Carry it flat, on a firm surface, with the air conditioning on.

Cutting and serving

Use a long, clean knife, dipped in hot water between cuts.

Questions

How far ahead should I order?

On this sample page, at least 7 days. A real business should state its own notice.

Do you make gluten-free or lactose-free cakes?

Only as adaptations on request, with no guarantees. We work with gluten and milk, and because of cross-contamination we never promise a cake that is “free from” anything.

Can I ask for a character from a show or film?

No. We make our own designs, with no licensed characters. Tell us what you like and we will turn it into an original cake.

Do they contain alcohol?

Some fillings and sponges may. We state it in the order so you can decide.

How long does the cake last?

It depends on the cake, the temperature and the journey. When we hand it over, we explain how to store it.

How is the order confirmed?

In writing, by WhatsApp or email: flavours, servings, allergies, date and final price.

Allergens. The studio works with gluten, egg, milk and tree nuts, among others. Because of cross-contamination, no cake is promised to be “free from” anything. If you have allergies, tell us when you order and we will confirm in writing. “Gluten-free” or “lactose-free” versions are only adaptations on request, with no guarantees.
