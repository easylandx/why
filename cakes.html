The problem with the usual options

Website builders were made for everyone. That's exactly the problem.

A drag-and-drop template can't tell a naked cake from a fondant sculpture, and it definitely can't show off the difference between your everyday cupcakes and your six-tier wedding centerpiece. Bakers end up with a site that looks like a florist's, or a gym's, with their cake photos dropped in as an afterthought.

Generic templates flatten your style

Whimsical buttercream florals and sleek modern fondant get squeezed into the same rounded card layout as a plumber's homepage.

Ordering is buried in menus

Custom cakes aren't an "add to cart" product — they need a flavor, size, tier count, and event date, and most builders can't ask for that cleanly.

Photos load slow, look flat

Your cake photography is the whole pitch. It deserves a page built to make it load fast and look sharp on every phone.
