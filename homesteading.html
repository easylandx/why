Hi, I'm Elena

I started with three pots of tomatoes and today I live on what I grow, preserve and ferment. For eight years I've been teaching people in several countries to do the same, from a balcony to a farm. I won't sell you a perfect life: I teach you to start with what you have, fail cheaply and move one step each season.

What I teach

Gardening and seeds, compost and water, hens, bees, preserves, ferments and sourdough bread. All practical, hands in the soil.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need land?

No. You can start on a balcony and grow at your own pace.

How much does it cost to start?

With 50 to 100 euros you get pots, soil, seeds and a first compost. I make you a custom list.

How much time a week?

From 3 hours on a balcony to 25 on a farm. We work it out together.

Will it work in my climate?

Yes. I adapt the calendar and species to your area, altitude and hemisphere.
