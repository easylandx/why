About me

I grew up between the sea and the cork oaks of the Alentejo, where nothing was thrown away. Years later, in a city full of packaging, I started making my own soaps and wraps in my kitchen. Today I make products in my workshop that last, can be refilled or returned, and I ship them to homes in several countries. I won’t ask for a perfect home: I suggest changing one thing at a time, with honest products.

Questions

What’s in each product?

The full ingredient list is on the label and on each product page.

How long do wraps and soaps last?

A wrap washed in cold water lasts about a year. A soap lasts weeks, depending on use.

How do refills work?

You send me the empty jar, I clean it and send it back full.

Do you ship outside Europe?

Yes, worldwide, in cardboard and paper packaging. Delivery time and cost depend on the country.

Do you sell wholesale?

Yes, to shops, hotels and guesthouses. Write to me and I’ll prepare a proposal.
