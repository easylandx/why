Questions

Is this a medical therapy?

No. These are complementary relaxation and wellbeing practices. They do not diagnose or treat any condition.

Do I need any previous experience?

No. Just a wish to slow down for a while. I explain everything as we go.

What should I wear?

Comfortable, loose clothes. For massage we provide towels and your privacy is respected at all times.

What if I am pregnant or take medication?

Check first with your healthcare professional and tell Noa before starting. Some practices should be adapted or avoided.

What if I need to cancel?

With 24 hours’ notice it can be moved at no cost. This is an example condition: a real centre must set its own.

Does reiki heal?

No. It is offered only as a relaxation practice. It does not replace any treatment or promise health effects.
