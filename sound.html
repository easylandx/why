About me

I grew up in Yogyakarta, where the gamelan plays at every celebration, and I learned that sound can also hold us. For nine years I have guided sound baths in different countries, with gongs, bowls and the instruments of my homeland. I don’t promise cures: I create a safe space where the body can slow down and rest. All you have to do is arrive, lie down and listen.

“Laras” is the Javanese word for the tuning of the gamelan.

What is a sound bath

You lie down, close your eyes and sound surrounds you for 60 to 90 minutes. You don’t have to do anything or know how to meditate. Many people feel calm, sleepy, or simply quiet inside.
