Frequently asked questions

What does the price include?

The lesson with your instructor. Lift pass and equipment hire are extra.

It is my first time, can I join?

Of course! We start from the very beginning on gentle terrain and move at your pace.

What is the minimum age?

Children can join from age 5. An adult makes the booking for anyone under 18.

What happens if the weather changes?

If the resort closes the slopes for your lesson, we offer another date or a refund.

Where do we meet?

In Sierra Nevada. Once your booking is confirmed, you receive the exact spot and time.

See you on the slopes?

Tell us which day you are coming and we will get your lesson ready.
