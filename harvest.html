Frequently asked questions

How do I prevent botulism in home preserves?

The toxin can be present even when look, smell and taste seem normal. Low-acid vegetables, pulses, meat and fish need a pressure canner or autoclave and a validated process; at home, freezing them is safest. Acid or properly acidified foods can be water-bath processed with a tested recipe. Throw away, without tasting, any jar that is bulging, rusty, leaking, unsealed or smells odd. If you have blurred vision, trouble swallowing or speaking, or weakness, get emergency medical help (112 in the EU).

Can I reuse jars?

The glass, yes, if it is intact with no cracks or chips. Use new lids for each batch, as the seal loses its grip, and wash and sterilise the jar before filling.

Can I refreeze thawed food?

My house rule: if you thawed raw food in the fridge, cook it, then freeze the dish. If it thawed outside the fridge, do not refreeze it, and if it sat for hours at room temperature, throw it away.

Use-by or best-before?

Use-by is about safety: after that date, throw the food away. Best-before is about quality: afterwards it may still be fine if it was stored as the label says, the pack is intact and its condition is good.
