Our craft

Four ways to make food last

Every ingredient has a method it prefers. We design the storage system around the food, not the other way round.

Water-bath & pressure canning

Jams, sauces and low-acid vegetables sealed for one to five years on a shelf.

Freeze-drying

Removes nearly all moisture, keeping full flavor and nutrients for 15–25 years.

Vacuum sealing

Pulls air out to slow oxidation — grains, meat and dried goods stay fresh far longer.

Root cellaring

Cool, humid, dark storage that keeps root vegetables firm for months without power.
