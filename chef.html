Questions

What does the per-person price include?

Food, cooking and service. What each pack covers is confirmed in writing.

Can you cook around allergies?

Yes, if you tell us beforehand. What we can guarantee is confirmed in writing.

What do you need at home?

A kitchen with hob or stove, a fridge and space to plate. If something is missing, we talk it through first.

Who sets up and clears?

We do, depending on the service you choose. It is stated in the quote.

Can I cancel?

Example cancellation terms appear in the quote. A real business would set its own.

Is the menu fixed?

No. Dishes vary with the market and the season, and are agreed with you.
