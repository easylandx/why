About me

I grew up in Lucerne among grandmothers' stories and chocolate bars, and trained as a confectioner in Zurich. I spent eighteen years in a large factory until I wanted to know where each bean truly came from. Eleven years later, I buy cacao directly from friendly farms, roast it, refine it for days and turn it into chocolate in my small workshop by the lake. My idea is simple: patience, precision and every bite telling where it comes from.

Frequently asked questions

Are there allergen traces?

How do you ship in summer?

With insulated packaging and on cooler days.

How do I store chocolate?

In a cool, dry place, between 15 and 18 degrees.

Do you take event orders?

Yes, bonbon boxes and bars with your message.

About two weeks from confirmation.
