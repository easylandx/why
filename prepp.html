About me

I was born in Leipzig and have lived in Madrid for eleven years. In Germany I grew up with the ordinary idea of keeping a backup pantry; here I noticed almost nobody did, until the great blackout of April 2025 left half the peninsula with warm fridges and no signal.

Since then I coach families, couples and people living alone to prepare with common sense: a few boxes under the bed, some bottles of water and a plan that fits on one sheet of paper. I don’t sell fear, I sell peace of mind.

What I teach

Six areas, one at a time. Each is solved with cheap things and space you already have.

Water

How much you need, how to store and refresh it, and what to do if the tap stops.

Pantry

Rice, pasta, lentils and cans you actually like, rotated so nothing expires.

Under-bed drawer

First-aid kit, flashlight, blankets and water in a spot your home already has.

Light and radio

Lanterns, headlamps, batteries and a radio to hear what’s going on.

Papers and contacts

Copies of documents, a little cash and phone numbers on paper.

Heat, cold and mindset

How to get through a day without power with kids, pets or elders, without drama.

Reserve calculator

    Frequently asked questions

    Is this for people who are afraid?

    No. It’s for people who prefer not to improvise. Many clients describe it as a weight off their shoulders.

    Do I need much space?

    No. A drawer, a cupboard or a shelf is enough to begin.

    How much does it cost to start?

    40 to 60 euros gives you a reasonable base. The rest is adding bit by bit.

    What if I live in a small flat?

    That’s the most common case. There are tricks for storing water and food discreetly.

    Is it legal to store all this?

    Water, food, batteries and first aid, yes. I won’t coach anything beyond that.
