The four systems

Off-grid isn't one skill. It's four systems working together — energy, water, food, and shelter — and coaching means sizing each one correctly for your household, not guessing.

Energy

Solar array and battery bank sized to your actual daily load, not a brochure estimate.

Water

Catchment, filtration, and storage that carries a household through a dry month.

Food

Growing space, preservation, and small livestock scaled to your land and appetite.

Shelter

Insulation, siting, and materials matched to your climate before anything else is built.
