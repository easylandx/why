Hi, I'm Marta

At 43 I left the city with a laptop, two solar panels and a lot of doubts. Today, nine years later, I live in a cabin with my own water and energy and work from it. I guide people and families in several countries through their own transition, at their pace and with numbers in hand. I won't promise a life without effort: I help you decide well, avoid costly mistakes and test before you leap.

Frequently asked questions

Is it legal to live off-grid?

It depends on the country and municipality. I teach you how to check what your area allows before you buy or build.

How much does it cost to start?

A small cabin with basic energy and water can start at around 15,000 euros, not counting the land. We build your budget together.

How do I work from there?

With satellite or mobile internet and well-planned remote work. We cover it in month 9.

What if it goes wrong?

That is why we test first, rehearse winter and keep an exit open.
