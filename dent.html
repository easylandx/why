First visit

What happens on day one.

The unknown is what scares people. So we walk you through it, step by step.

  1. We welcome you

    No waiting, no rush. We explain what we are going to do before we start.

  2. A full check-up

    We examine your teeth and gums and, if needed, take a digital X-ray.

  3. A clear plan and price

    We show you what we see on screen and give you a written quote.

  4. You decide

    No obligation. If you want to start, we book you in. If not, you leave with your report.
