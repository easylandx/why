What it is not

Three clear ideas before you begin.

It is not sleep

During a session you stay aware, hear everything and can open your eyes or stop whenever you like.

It is not losing control

Nobody can make you say or do anything against your will. You decide at every moment.

It is not a treatment

It does not replace medical or psychological care and is not offered as a solution to any health problem.
